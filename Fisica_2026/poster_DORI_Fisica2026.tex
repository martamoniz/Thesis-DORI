\begin{minipage}[t]{\colA}\topcol
  \begin{doriblock}[height=\rowonehgt]{ABSTRACT}

Interventional radiology is the medical specialty subject to the highest
occupational exposure to ionising radiation. Monitoring still relies on
passive dosimeters, which report dose retrospectively, so exposure accidents
cannot be prevented.

This work presents DORI (\textbf{\textit{DO}}s\'imetro para
\textbf{\textit{R}}adiologia de \textbf{\textit{I}}nterven\c{c}\~ao), a wireless
active personal dosimeter for real-time monitoring of scattered radiation. The
sensing element is a 5\,$\times$\,5\,$\times$\,5\,mm$^3$ BC-408 plastic
scintillator coupled to a Broadcom SiPM with a
6\,$\times$\,6\,mm$^2$ active area. The readout electronics is controlled by an
ESP32-C3 powered by a rechargeable Li-Po battery. Energy calibration was
performed with bremsstrahlung endpoints and the $^{241}$Am photopeak, with the
$^{57}$Co Compton edge as an independent validation point. The dose response
followed ISO 4037 narrow spectra in terms of $H_p(10)$. The prototype is
compact, fully wireless and unobtrusive to wear.
  \end{doriblock}

%  METHODS / Energy Calibration
%    Bremsstrahlung endpoints acquired between 20 and 50 kVp, combined with the
%    Am-241 photopeak at 59.5 keV in a weighted least-squares fit. The Co-57
%    Compton edge was used as an independent validation point.
%
%  METHODS / Dose Calibration
%    Calibration followed ISO 4037, using the narrow-spectrum qualities N-25,
%    N-30 and N-40 and the prescribed protocol for Hp(10) [3,4]. The dose is
%    reconstructed as  Hp(10) = w1 N1 + w2 N2 , with Ni the counts in each
%    energy bin and wi the fitted weights.
%
%  DISCUSSION
%    The energy response is linear over the 20 to 60 keV range. The Co-57 point
%    falls off the line, owing to the poor energy resolution at this energy.
%    The dose results support the two-bin division, given the small number of
%    radiation qualities available.
%    The designed architecture is scalable to other formats, and its current
%    autonomy, dimensions and mass allow unobtrusive operation, with room for
%    improvement in future iterations.
